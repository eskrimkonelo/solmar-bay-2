SOLMAR BAY

Solmar Bay — a top-down open-world crime game. Explore a sun-soaked coastal city on foot and by car, steal cars, dodge the police, and work through six story missions. Move with WASD or the arrow keys, aim with the mouse, click to shoot, E to enter or leave a car, Shift to run, P or Escape to pause, M to mute.
